Privacy policy

Dryport offsite backup is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

It asks Google for the drive.file permission only: it can see and change only the files it creates in its owner's Google Drive, nothing else there.

It collects, stores and shares no personal data about anyone. The files it writes are the owner's own backups, encrypted before they leave the owner's servers.

Contact: the GitHub account vladdran.
